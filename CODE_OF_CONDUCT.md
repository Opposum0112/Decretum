# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Decretum is an open technical project. We welcome participation from people with different backgrounds, experience levels and perspectives.

We expect contributors to communicate professionally, assume good faith, and focus criticism on ideas and implementations rather than people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and other abusive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code.

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainers through the repository's private security/contact channel rather than opening a public issue.
